Tip (Windows): press Win + Alt + R to record the screen with the Xbox Game Bar,
or use the Snipping Tool's video option. Trim in the Photos app.

IMPORTANT - FILE FORMAT: save clips as MP4 (H.264). Chrome and Edge on
Windows will NOT play iPhone HEVC / .mov clips. On an iPhone set
Settings > Camera > Formats > "Most Compatible" before recording, or
convert the clip to MP4 before copying it here.
