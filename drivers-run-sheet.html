On Zoom: click Open projector window, leave it open (not minimised) and in Zoom choose Share screen → that window. Short of time? Skip slides 7 and 11, run the breakout for 3 minutes and skip the AI summary: about 23 minutes. Zoom then shows only the talk and the live results; this run sheet, your notes and the presenter controls stay private. Paste the join link into the Zoom chat so people on laptops can answer too; people with phones can scan the code on screen. Walls and votes open on phones by themselves when you reach their slide. The flip cards, tabs and pickers are clicked directly in the projector window, which is fine to do while sharing.

In a room with a projector: Click Open projector window, drag that window onto the projector and click its full-screen button. From then on the projector only ever shows the talk or the live results; this run sheet, your notes and the presenter controls stay on your laptop. Use ◀ ▶ (or your clicker, with this page in front) to move slides.

Each button opens or jumps the right screen: blue buttons move the talk to that slide, gold buttons open the presenter screen (votes and walls), green buttons show a website or the toolkit on the projector (or a laptop tab if the projector isn't open; ↗ always opens a laptop tab). Click Show talk to come back. Tap a section heading to mark where you are. About 20 minutes plus optional extras.
