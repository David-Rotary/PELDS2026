Why a new approach

Campaigns, targets and membership drives haven't turned it around here. People didn't stop caring. The way they give their time changed, and our invitation didn't.

The Board behind the Chair

Membership grows when the President and the Membership Chair work as a team, and the Board backs them.

The President

  • Membership near the top of every Board agenda
  • Personally invites two people to a project this year
  • Protects time and budget for the plan
  • Models the culture: notices people, thanks people

The Membership Chair

  • Leads the club's annual membership plan
  • Runs onboarding: a contact and induction within 90 days
  • Check-ins at 3, 6 and 12 months
  • Keeps the numbers: joins, departures, members at risk

Together, with the Board

  • One plan and one goal in Rotary Club Central
  • A quarterly review at the Board
  • Ten minutes of membership conversation every meeting
  • A shared answer: what will members say about this club on 30 June?

Take it home

Three ways to start the conversation in your club. Pick one.

10 minutes

A card at every Board meeting

Draw a conversation card on screen or from the printed pack, talk for ten minutes. Note one thing to try.

40 minutes

President and Membership Chair, together

Work through the partnership module, then show it to your Board in Board mode.

90 minutes

A club night: Grow your club, grow your people

The whole club votes, talks at tables and writes its own one-page plan on the night.

Then:Try one small thing for 90 days→Review it at the Board→Share what you learned with other clubs
Make the choice feel small. The 10-minute card is enough to start. The club night is the big one: it ends with the club's own plan, written by its own members, which is the opposite of a District target. "Meet Sam" is also there as a 20-minute talk for a club meeting.

The ask

We're looking for people to drive this in their club.

Not a committee. Not a title. Just someone who'll start the conversation and keep it going.

What a driver does
  1. Starts one conversation in your club before the end of March: a card, the module or a club night
  2. Tries one small thing for 90 days and notices what changes
  3. Joins the drivers' circle: 30 minutes on Zoom, once a month, to swap what's working
  4. Reports back in a minute when the reminder pops up, so the next club can borrow what worked
