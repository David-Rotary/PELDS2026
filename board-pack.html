Agenda item: ten minutes of membership

A standing item, near the top of the agenda, every meeting. The Membership Chair leads; the President protects the time.

1 min
The numbersMembers today, joins and leavers since last meeting, anyone at risk. Once a year, bring the club snapshot.
6 min
One cardSomeone draws a card and reads it aloud. Hear from everyone before anyone suggests a fix. No right answers, only honest ones.
2 min
One thing to tryWhat's one small thing we could try before the next meeting? Who'll do it, and by when?
1 min
Last time's thingDid we try it? What happened? “Not yet” is a fine answer.
