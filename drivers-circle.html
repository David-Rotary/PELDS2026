The drivers' circle

Thirty minutes on Zoom, once a month. Nobody presents. Two clubs share what they tried, we talk over one card, and everyone leaves with one small next step.

← Drivers' kit

How 30 minutes runs

0–5
Did you try it?Open the presenter screen, session "Membership: It's in your hands", and run the vote Did you try it? Then show What clubs are trying for a minute. "Not yet" is a perfectly good answer.
5–15
Two stories, five minutes eachAsk two drivers in advance. Three questions only: what did you try, what happened, what would you tell another club? Others ask one question each, no advice yet.
15–25
One conversation cardDraw a card from the Board pack or the Explorer and use this month's theme (below). Small breakout rooms of three if there are more than eight people. Hear from everyone before anyone suggests a fix.
25–30
One small next step eachEveryone types theirs in the chat. Encourage them to make it a commitment with a reminder on the drivers' kit page, so the reminder brings them back to report.

This month's theme

Follows the Membership Hub's year planner, so the circle talks about what Membership Chairs are doing anyway.

If it's your turn to host

A week before

  • Send the invitation below with the Zoom link
  • Ask two drivers to share a story (five minutes each)
  • Look at What clubs are trying on the presenter screen, so you know what's come in

Afterwards

  • Post the next steps from the chat to the drivers (first names only)
  • Encourage the two storytellers to share their story so it reaches the District roundup
  • Pick next month's host before you hang up

Circle rules

Stories, not reports. What happened, not what the Board minuted.
No names of members or prospects. First names of drivers are fine.
Small is good. "We tried one card at one Board meeting" counts.
